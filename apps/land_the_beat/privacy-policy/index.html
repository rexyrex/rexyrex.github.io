Land the Beat privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Land the Beat is a rhythm and pitch game from Rexyrex. You can use it without an account.

Information on your device

The game saves scores, practice progress, tap calibration, language and other settings on your device. It uses those details to run the game and personalize your practice. You can clear your progress from Settings. The game generates its sounds locally and does not record audio or use your microphone.

Ads and app diagnostics

  • Google AdMob displays banner ads and optional rewarded video ads. Google may collect IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or advertising identifiers, app or ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information to serve, measure, and help prevent fraud in ads. The app does not request access to your device location.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand app use through information such as app sessions, interactions, app and device details, and installation identifiers.

  • Firebase Crashlytics helps us diagnose crashes using information such as stack traces, device and app details, and installation identifiers. The app does not send your locally saved scores or tap calibration as part of its own analytics events.

Google may process these details on our behalf or for the purposes described in its partner privacy notice and Firebase privacy notice. Google processes information under the retention rules for the corresponding service; for example, Firebase states that it keeps Crashlytics reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting deletion.

Your choices

If shown, use Settings → Ad privacy to change available ad consent preferences. On iOS, you can also change tracking permission under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can manage your advertising ID in your device’s Ads settings. You can decline an optional rewarded ad and return to setup or choose a different test.

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. For questions about this policy or to exercise a privacy right that applies to you, email rexyrex.dev@gmail.com.
