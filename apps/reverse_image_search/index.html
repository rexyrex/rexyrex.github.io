Where your photo goes

A reverse image search engine has to see the picture, so the app has to share it. This is exactly how.

  1. On your phone

    The photo you choose is resized so its longest side is at most 2048 px.

  2. A temporary image host

    It’s uploaded to Litterbox (by Catbox), or to ImgBB if that upload fails, with a 24-hour expiry requested. The host returns a public link, and anyone who has that link can open the image while it’s up.

  3. Four search engines

    The link goes to Google Lens, TinEye, Yandex Images and Bing Visual Search. Their pages open in built-in browsers, where they see the link, your IP address and browser details, and may set cookies and show their own ads.

Rexyrex runs no image server and keeps no copy of your uploads. The 24-hour setting can’t control copies a search provider keeps, so choose only photos you’re comfortable sharing with these services.

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, never on your first search. On iOS it asks before allowing tracking, and uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, whose events don’t include your photos, image links or searches.
