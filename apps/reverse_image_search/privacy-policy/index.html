Privacy Policy for Reverse Image Search

Effective Date: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 김민형 (also known as rexyrex, "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Reverse Image Search mobile application (the "App"). Reverse Image Search is a utility that lets you select or capture a photo and look it up across major visual search engines.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you actively provide (for example, by choosing a photo to search), and information that is collected automatically when you use the App or when third-party SDKs operate in the background.

1.1 Photos and Camera Content

When you tap the upload area, you can choose an image from your device's photo library or capture a new one with your camera. The selected image is read by the App, optionally resized for transmission, and uploaded to a temporary image-hosting service so that the visual search engines can fetch it by URL. We do not retain copies of these images on any servers we control.

1.2 Temporarily Uploaded Images

To enable URL-based reverse image search, the App uploads your selected image to one of the following temporary image hosts:

  • Litterbox (catbox.moe) — primary upload target, with a 24-hour expiration set by the App.
  • ImgBB — fallback upload target, used only if the primary upload fails, with a 24-hour expiration set by the App.

These services receive your image so that they can return a public URL. The temporary URL is then passed to the visual search engines listed below. After 24 hours, the upload expires and is removed by the host according to its own policies.

1.3 WebView Browsing Activity

After upload, the App opens search-result pages from third-party visual search engines inside an in-app web browser (WebView). These engines load and render your image and may set cookies, log queries, and apply their own analytics, exactly as if you had visited their websites in a regular browser. The engines used are:

  • Google Lens (lens.google.com)
  • TinEye (tineye.com)
  • Yandex Images (yandex.com)
  • Bing Visual Search (bing.com)

Browsing activity inside the WebView is governed by each provider's privacy policy, not ours.

1.4 Advertising Identifiers

The App displays interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may access advertising identifiers — the Advertising ID (AAID) on Android and, where allowed, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS — along with general device information, coarse location inferred from IP, and ad-interaction data, in order to deliver and measure ads. On iOS, the App does not currently display the App Tracking Transparency prompt, so AdMob operates without access to the IDFA and serves non-personalized ads on that platform.

You can reset or disable advertising identifiers at any time:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads.

1.5 Locally Stored Preferences

The App uses on-device storage (shared_preferences) to remember a small counter — the number of searches you have performed — which is used to skip the interstitial ad on your very first search. This data lives only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

1.6 Automatically Collected Technical Information

When the App makes network requests (image uploads, ad calls, font requests, WebView page loads), the receiving servers automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address, device type, operating-system version, and timestamp. We do not separately log this information ourselves.

1.7 Font Assets

The App uses the google_fonts package, which fetches typography assets from the Google Fonts service the first time a font is needed. Google Fonts may log standard request metadata (IP address, user agent) when serving these assets.

6. Temporary Image Hosting

Once the host's expiration window passes, the file is no longer retrievable. Please consider this before uploading photos that you do not wish to be temporarily accessible by URL.
