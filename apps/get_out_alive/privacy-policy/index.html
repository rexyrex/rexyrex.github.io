Get Out Alive privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Get Out Alive is a single-player scenario game from Rexyrex. You can play without an account. The game saves endings, scenario unlocks, language and other settings on your device. Your saved choices and progress are not sent to a game server.

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock a scenario, the app uses Google AdMob. AdMob may collect IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The app does not request access to your device location. Google processes this information according to its partner privacy notice.

If available, use the privacy icon on the title screen to review or change your ad privacy choices. On iOS, you can also change tracking permission under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can manage your advertising ID in your device’s Ads settings. You can decline a rewarded ad and continue playing the scenarios that are already available to you.

For privacy questions or requests, email rexyrex.dev@gmail.com.
