1. Information We Collect

Some information is collected automatically when you use the app, and some is created or accessed only when you take a specific action (such as scanning a code or saving an image). We have designed QR Scanner to keep as much of your data as possible on your own device.

1.1 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the app, our analytics and crash-reporting providers may automatically collect technical information such as your device model, operating system version, app version, language and region settings, general usage events (for example, which screens are opened), session duration, and crash diagnostics. This information is used to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. It is not used to identify you personally.

1.2 Camera Information

QR Scanner uses your device camera to detect and decode QR codes and barcodes in real time. The camera feed is processed on your device to read codes. We do not record, store, or transmit camera images or video. Camera access is requested only when you use the scanning feature, and you can deny or revoke this permission in your device settings.

1.3 Photos and Media

If you choose to scan a QR code from an existing image, the app accesses the specific photo you select from your photo library so it can be cropped and decoded on your device. If you generate a QR image and choose to save it, the app adds that image to your photo library. We do not read or upload your broader photo library, and the images you select are processed only on your device.

1.4 Scan History and Locally Stored Data

The QR codes and barcodes you scan, along with their timestamps, are stored in a local database on your device so you can browse and search your scan history. The app also stores app preferences locally. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is removed when you delete the relevant entries or uninstall the app.

1.5 Advertising Identifiers

The app displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may access advertising identifiers (the IDFA on iOS and the Advertising ID on Android) and limited interaction data to deliver and measure ads. See the "Advertising and Tracking" section below for details and your choices.
