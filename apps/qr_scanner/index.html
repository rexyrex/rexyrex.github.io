/apps/qr_scanner/#history

Every scan saved, and easy to find again

Scans are saved on their own, newest first, with the date and time. Search by what a code says, or tap a type to see only links, Wi-Fi networks, contacts, emails, phone numbers or text.

Reopen anything
Tap a scan to open it again, copy it, show it as a QR code, share it or delete it.
Tidy up
Select several scans and delete them together, or clear the whole history.
Kept on your phone
History lives in a database on your device, and stays there.

In the app

Save puts the code in your photo gallery, Share sends it anywhere, and Copy QR content copies the text inside it. Exports are a 1024-pixel code with its white border, as PNG or JPG. The download above uses the same size and border.

Templates fill in the fiddly formats for you: a Wi-Fi code guests scan to join, a contact card, a pre-filled SMS or WhatsApp message, a map location, an email with subject and body.

/apps/qr_scanner/privacy-policy/

What stays on your phone

  • Your scan history. It’s kept in a database on your device and never sent to Rexyrex. Delete scans one at a time or clear them all.
  • The camera. Frames are read on the device to find codes. Nothing is recorded or stored.
  • No account. There’s nothing to sign up for.

QR Scanner is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and it uses Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. The privacy policy has the details. The generator on this page runs in your browser: what you type into it isn’t sent anywhere.
