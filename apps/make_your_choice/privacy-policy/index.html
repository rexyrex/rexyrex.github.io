Privacy Policy for Make Your Choice

Effective Date: 2026-11-07

Make Your Choice is a silly decision-tree game of terrible choices, made by Rexyrex. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics of its own.

Stored on your device

Your game progress (chapters cleared, endings found, bonus chapters unlocked) and your settings (sound, language, the character you play as) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Bonus chapters can be unlocked by choosing to watch a rewarded video ad. These ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its ad partners may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), where you allow it,

  • your IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,

  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), and

  • how you interact with the ad.

This is used to deliver, measure and limit ads, and, where you have agreed, to personalise them. The app itself does not request location permission.

2. Your Choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): you are asked for consent through Google’s consent message before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options.

  • Tracking on iOS: the app asks permission before accessing your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

  • Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. You can play every free chapter without seeing any ads.
