Privacy Policy for Rex Remote

Effective Date: 2026-10-26

Rex Remote shows live statistics, history, widgets and alerts for Linux, macOS and Windows servers that you add yourself. It connects directly from your device to those servers over SSH. There is no Rex Remote account, no Rex Remote server and no cloud service in between.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information. Rex Remote contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking code, and the developer never receives data from the app.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • The app’s SSH key. On first use the app generates its own ed25519 key pair. The private key is stored in the operating system’s secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore-backed storage) and never leaves the device.

  • Your server list. The names, addresses, ports, user names and pinned host keys of the servers you add, and your alert and display settings.

  • Recent readings. The latest statistics for each server, which the home screen widgets and alerts read.

If you choose the one-time login when adding a server, the password or private key you enter is used for that single connection to install the app’s restricted key on the server, and is not stored.

Removing a server in the app deletes its entry and readings from the device. Uninstalling the app deletes the rest; on iOS the system may keep the app’s key in the Keychain after an uninstall, and the app reuses it if you install it again.

3. Connections the App Makes

The app connects only to the servers you add, using SSH, from your device. On those servers it can only start the Rex Boing agent: the key it authorises is restricted so that it cannot open a shell or forward connections. The setup command the app shows you downloads the agent from the project’s public GitHub releases page; that download is made by your server, not by the app.

In demo mode the app replays bundled sample recordings and opens no network connections at all.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not share, sell or transfer any information, because the app sends none to us or to any third party. When you use the share button, the text you share goes only where you choose to send it.

5. Notifications and Background Refresh

If you allow notifications, alerts about your servers are created on your device from readings the app fetched itself. Background refresh, when allowed by your operating system, makes the same direct SSH connections to your servers to keep widgets and alerts current.

6. Children’s Privacy

Rex Remote is a tool for server administrators and is not directed to children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Data Security

Connections to your servers are encrypted with SSH, and each server’s host key is pinned so that a changed key stops the connection. The app’s private key is kept in the operating system’s secure storage.

8. Your Rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove what the app stores by removing your servers in the app or uninstalling it. To take the app’s access away from a server, use the app’s option to uninstall the agent from that server, or delete the app’s line from the server’s authorized_keys file.
