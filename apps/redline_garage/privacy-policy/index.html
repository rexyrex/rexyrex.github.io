Privacy Policy for Redline Garage

Effective Date: 2026-11-07

Redline Garage is an engine sound simulator: pick an engine, tune it, hear every change and run the drag strip. This policy explains what information the app and its service providers handle, and why. By using Redline Garage you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Redline Garage has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or location.

1.1 Automatically Collected Information

The app collects technical information automatically, including device model, operating system version, app version, language, and anonymous usage events (for example which screens you open, which engine you unlock, and drag-strip run results). Crash and diagnostic reports include stack traces and the state of the app at the moment of failure.

1.2 Advertising Identifiers

The app shows optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch to unlock PRO engines. When an ad is requested, Google AdMob may access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID on Android), your IP address (from which approximate region may be inferred) and basic interaction data to deliver, measure and limit ads. On iOS, the advertising identifier is only available if you allow tracking when asked.

1.3 Data Stored on Your Device

Your settings, tunes, unlocked engines and best drag-strip times are stored only on your device using the operating system’s standard storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve Redline Garage’s features

  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes and bugs

  • To understand how the app is used so we can prioritize improvements

  • To deliver and measure the rewarded ads that unlock PRO engines

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where required, we rely on your consent (for example for personalized ads), and otherwise on our legitimate interest in operating, securing and improving the app. You can withdraw consent at any time as described in Section 5.

  • Consent: in regions that require it (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.
