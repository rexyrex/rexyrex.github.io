DM 기록의 내용은 AdMob에 전달되지 않으며, 메시지 내용을 보여 주는 화면(단어 검색, 퀴즈, 날짜별 대화 보기)에는 광고를 표시하지 않습니다. Google의 데이터 처리 방식은 Google 서비스를 사용하는 사이트 또는 앱의 정보를 Google이 사용하는 방법에서 확인할 수 있습니다.

This privacy policy explains how “DMA - DM Analyzer” (“the app”), a mobile app provided by Rexyrex (“the developer”), handles information. The app analyzes Instagram direct-message (DM) histories that you export, and shows statistics, all on your device. The app is unofficial and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc. Instagram is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc.

1. DM histories (data you provide)

  • The only chat data the app handles is the ZIP file you download with Instagram’s “Export your information” (JSON format) and hand to the app through the share sheet or the file picker.

  • From it, the app reads only the message files and the profile file (to recognize your own name). Photos, videos and the other files in the export are never opened.

  • The message files contain participants’ display names, message text, timestamps, reactions, links to shared posts and reels, and call records.

  • Reading, analysis and storage all happen on your device. Your DM histories and their analysis results are never sent to the developer or to any third-party server. The app has no upload feature and no accounts.

  • The imported messages and results are kept in the app’s private storage (which other apps cannot read) so you can reopen them later.

  • They are erased when you delete a chat, choose “Delete all data” in Settings, or uninstall the app.

2. Shared images and text

Result images and text are created on your device and are shared only when you pick a destination in your system’s share sheet. “Hide names in shared images” in Settings replaces names with A, B, and so on. DM histories include other people’s information, so please take care when sharing.

3. Settings stored on your device

The app stores your theme, language, the hide-names setting, features you unlocked by watching an ad, and which participant is “me” in each chat. These never leave your device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, limit how often they appear, and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and use:

  • the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only with your permission; the Android advertising ID)

  • your IP address and the approximate location inferred from it

  • technical information such as device model, operating system and language

  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps

The content of your DM histories is never passed to AdMob, and the app never shows ads on screens that display message text (word search, quiz, day viewer). For how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Consent and tracking

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your ad consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) and requests ads according to your choice.

  • On iOS, the app asks for permission to use the advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, the IDFA is not used.

  • You can reset or limit the advertising identifier at any time (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Usage analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google LLC, to understand how the app is used and to find and fix crashes.

  • Analytics automatically records basic usage, such as app opens, sessions and their length, screens viewed, the app version, device model, operating system, language, and the country or region derived from your IP address, together with an identifier that Firebase creates for each installation (the app instance ID). On Android, Analytics may also read the Android advertising ID; on iOS it does not use the IDFA.

  • When the app crashes or hits a serious error, Crashlytics receives a crash report: the stack trace, device model, operating system and app versions, technical state such as free memory and disk space, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Crash reports are sent only from release versions of the app.

  • Firebase never receives your DM content. No message text, participant names, chat titles, export files, analysis results or shared images are sent to Firebase, and the app records no custom events, user IDs or user properties.

  • This information is used only to improve the app’s stability and features. It is not used for advertising or to track you, and it is not sold. Firebase processes it as a service provider to the developer.

See Firebase privacy and security for details.

7. What the app does not collect

The app does not collect account information, contacts or photos, and does not request location permission.

8. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not hold your DM histories or any other personal information, and does not sell or hand it to anyone. The only third parties that receive information are Google AdMob (Section 4) and Google Firebase (Section 6), and neither receives your DM content.

9. Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Information collected by AdMob is kept under Google’s policies. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for up to 90 days, and analytics data is kept under the data-retention setting of the app’s Google Analytics property.

10. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act or the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information. Because the developer does not hold your DM histories, you can erase the data on your device with the app’s delete options or by uninstalling it. Ad-related data can be managed in the device settings above and in your Google account settings. For anything else, contact us below.
