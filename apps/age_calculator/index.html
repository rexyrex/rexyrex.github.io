Five little tests, one big question

Pick any tile on the home screen. Every test ends the same way: with a number.

Hearing Test

A pure tone climbs from 200 Hz to 18 kHz over 30 seconds. Tap “I can't hear it!” the moment it fades out: the highest pitch you caught sets your age. Headphones or speakers both work.

Reaction Time

Eight rounds. After a random wait of one to four seconds the screen flashes green and you tap anywhere. Jump the gun and that round starts over.

Try five rounds in the booth

Pair Match

Sixteen cards show their faces for five seconds, then flip. Find all eight pairs. Speed and mistakes both count.

Vision Test

Hold your phone at arm's length while a word shrinks through 13 sizes, from 28 pt down to 2 pt. Say when it's too small to read.

Tap Trail

The numbers 1 to 25 are scattered across the screen. Tap them in order. The faster you finish, the younger the result.

The fine print, in big print

For fun, not a check-up

Results are playful estimates from how you play. They don't tell your real or biological age, and the hearing and vision tests are no substitute for a professional check. Volume, lighting, your device and practice all move the numbers.

What the app collects

Tests are scored on your phone, with no account to make. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage statistics and crash reports. Read the privacy policy.
