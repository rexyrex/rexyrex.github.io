/Applications/

Native on the Mac, menu bar first.

All Swift and SwiftUI. Rex Boing is open source; App Manager and RexDesk have download pages of their own. The laptop on the stump runs through four of them.

  • Rex BoingA tiny tyrannosaur in the menu bar that quickens as the Mac gets busier. Behind it, a dashboard: processor, GPU, memory, thermals, power, network, storage, and which process is to blame.macOS 14+ · MIT↗
  • App ManagerOne window for the whole app portfolio: store metadata and screenshots, fastlane deploys with live logs, store questionnaires, release state, revenue and health, and embedded agent sessions.macOS · Windows→
  • RexDeskControl a Mac from another Mac, or from any browser, as if you were sitting at it. Hardware video on both ends keeps text sharp and the pointer immediate, on the same network or over the internet, end-to-end encrypted.macOS 14+→
  • PMProject ManagerWatches every project folder, counts what is running, flags the duplicate dev servers an agent spawned and forgot, and builds or installs a Mac app in one click. Git status at a glance.In-house · Swift 6
  • AIAI Usage TrackerHow much Claude and Codex are left, from the menu bar: every usage window the signed-in accounts expose, a ring that shades toward red, and the slice used since the last refresh in amber.In-house · SwiftUI

~/teslamate-dashboard/

A dashboard for the car.

Live telemetry from TeslaMate over MQTT on a dark map with glass cards: speed, power, battery, climate, tire pressure, drive recaps and rolling charts. Plus full-screen visualizer scenes, an AI co-pilot that comments on the drive, and a few tiny games. Built to run in the car's own browser.

The red roadster flying the trail is the dot on that map. The dashboard itself is private; the picture is an illustration.
